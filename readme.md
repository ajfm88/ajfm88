## 🔎 About Me:

I started coding in college in [![C++](https://img.shields.io/badge/-C%2B%2B-00599C?style=flat&logo=c%2B%2B&logoColor=white)](https://isocpp.org), where I also took a database class using [![MySQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/MySQL-%2300758f.svg?logo=mysql&logoColor=ffffff)](https://mysql.com). I then went onto work as a coding instructor teaching [![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat&logo=python&logoColor=fff)](https://python.org) and basic [![HTML](https://img.shields.io/badge/-HTML-E34F26?&logo=html5&logoColor=ffffff)](https://html.spec.whatwg.org), [![CSS](https://img.shields.io/badge/-CSS-1572B6?&logo=css&logoColor=ffffff)](https://w3.org/Style/CSS) and [![JavaScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/-JavaScript-F7DF1E?&logo=javascript&logoColor=000000)](https://javascript.info) to school-age children from Elementary, Middle and High School.

After a year of self-learning, I was able to land a job as a Junior Software Developer where I went deep in [![MongoDB](https://img.shields.io/badge/MongoDB-47A248?style=flat&logo=mongodb&logoColor=white)](https://mongodb.com), [![Express](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Express-000000?logo=express&logoColor=fff&style=flat)](https://expressjs.com), [![Astro](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Astro-BC52EE?&logo=astro&logoColor=ffffff)](https://astro.build), [![Node.js](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Node.js-339933?&logo=nodedotjs&logoColor=ffffff)](https://nodejs.org), [![Visual Studio Code](https://custom-icon-badges.demolab.com/badge/Visual%20Studio%20Code-0078d7.svg?logo=vsc&logoColor=white)](https://code.visualstudio.com), [![Git](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Git-%23F05032?&logo=git&logoColor=%23ffffff)](https://git-scm.com), [![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/-GitHub-%231a202c?&logo=github&logoColor=ffffff)](https://github.com) and [![Linux](https://img.shields.io/badge/Linux-FCC624?style=flat&logo=linux&logoColor=black)](https://linux.org).

I also took the time to keep learning on my own and build portfolio pieces using [![TypeScript](https://img.shields.io/badge/-TypeScript-007ACC?&logo=typescript&logoColor=ffffff)](https://typescriptlang.org), [![Tailwind CSS](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Tailwind-06B6D4?&logo=tailwindcss&logoColor=ffffff)](https://tailwindcss.com), [![React](https://img.shields.io/badge/-React-61DAFB?&logo=react&logoColor=000000)](https://react.dev), [![PostgreSQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/PostgreSQL-336791?style=flat&logo=postgresql&logoColor=white)](https://postgresql.org) and [![Prisma](https://img.shields.io/badge/Prisma-2D3748?style=flat&logo=prisma&logoColor=white)](https://prisma.io)

Outside of code, I speak 🇺🇸 English, 🇻🇪/🇪🇸 Spanish, and I'm learning 🇯🇵 Japanese (JLPT N4). Fun fact: I'm a [![Yelp](https://img.shields.io/badge/-Yelp-C41200?&logo=Yelp&logoColor=FFFFFF)](https://yelp.com/user_details?userid=JBqCl4WE7g9SPR-0y0tJzQ) Elite Squad member who loves a good bowl of ramen 🍜. When I'm not coding, you'll find me focused on ✝️ my faith, 💪🏽 staying active, 📚 reading, or 📈 keeping an eye on the markets.
